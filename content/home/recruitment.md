I work on environmental, energy, and transportation economics, and I am recruiting at every level. The research is empirical and data-intensive: large administrative datasets, satellite data, and quasi-experimental designs. There is no fixed checklist for any of these roles. If your background is relevant and you are genuinely interested in these questions, please get in touch.

### Ph.D. students

I am looking for Ph.D. students to start in **Fall 2027**. Applications go through the University's official channels, but I am glad to hear from you before you apply. Students come from economics, statistics, environmental science, and engineering alike, and prior research experience is welcome but not expected.

### Master's students

I am also taking master's students for **Fall 2027**, and I supervise theses and research projects. This is a good fit if you want to produce original empirical work rather than coursework alone.

### Postdoctoral researchers

I anticipate recruiting a postdoctoral researcher to begin in **Fall 2027**. The position is meant to leave room for your own papers, not only to support mine. Length and funding depend on the project.

### Research assistants

I hire research assistants on a rolling basis, and **you can start at any time of year**, full-time or part-time, including part-time work alongside your studies at CUHK-Shenzhen. The work involves cleaning and linking large datasets, geospatial processing, and writing reproducible code. Experience with R, Stata, Python, or GIS helps, but careful, well-documented work matters more.

### How to apply

Email me at <hanxianru@cuhk.edu.cn> with a short note on why my research interests you, and please attach all of the following:

- your CV,
- your transcript,
- a working paper or a sample of your code.

Either option works for the last item: a paper if you have one, code if you do not. I read everything that arrives, though replies may take a week or two during the semester.
